OUR STORY

A Warm Cup of Comfort

Chiya Thana Cafe is more than just a place to drink tea. It is a cozy space where friends meet, conversations grow, and beautiful memories are created.

Located in Kalanki, we bring together the warmth of traditional Nepali chiya with delicious food and a peaceful atmosphere.

Whether you are spending time with friends, enjoying a quiet evening, or simply looking for a comfortable place to relax, Chiya Thana Cafe welcomes you.
